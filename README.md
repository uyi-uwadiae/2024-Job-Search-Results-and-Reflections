## What stood out

- **Narrow focus:** Strategy & Ops was 47.7% of applications, and San Francisco, Chicago, and New York took 79.1%.
- **Concentrated targets:** 10 companies received 251 applications (40.4%).
- **Volume didn't track results:** August's 130 applications produced 6 interview processes. September's 71 produced 7.
- **LinkedIn converted best:** 397 LinkedIn applications produced 31 interview processes (7.8%), versus 5 from 177 on company sites (2.8%).
- **Role fit mattered:** Strategy & Ops converted at 8.8% versus 4.0% for other functions. Product and marketing roles produced 3 interview processes from 150 applications (2.0%).
- **Long final rounds:** The 5 final-round processes involved 21 interviews, roughly 4 each, and none ended in an offer.

## Definitions and limits

- **Application:** one role logged in my tracker.
- **Response:** a denial or an interview.
- **Interview process:** an application that led to at least a recruiter screen.
- **Final round:** the last interview stage of a process.
- **Offer:** a job offer resulting from a logged application.
